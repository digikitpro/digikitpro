About This Product

Drawing realistic hair is one of the most time-consuming parts of any portrait.

Many artists struggle with the "stiff" look of digital hair or spend hours painting individual strands by hand. To get a natural, flowing look, you need tools that handle the heavy lifting of texture and volume.

The Professional Hair Kit provides a streamlined system for creating believable hair, and fine textures in Procreate. This set is designed to remove the frustration of manual hair-painting, allowing you to build volume and detail with precision.


What is included in this kit:

  • High-Precision Hair Stamps: Use these to establish the base flow and volume of the hair instantly.
  • Textured Strand Brushes: Create organic, flowing hair with natural pressure sensitivity.
  • Versatile Application: These brushes work in any color and opacity, giving you total control over the lighting and mood of your piece.


Why these brushes work:

  • Workflow Efficiency: Stop painting every single hair. Use stamps to build the form, then refine the details.
  • Apple Pencil Optimized: Full support for pressure and tilt, ensuring the brush reacts naturally to your hand.
  • Professional Results: Designed for concept artists and illustrators who need a clean, polished look without the hours of manual labor.
