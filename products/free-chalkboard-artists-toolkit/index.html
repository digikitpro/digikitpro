About This Product

Bring the grit and soul of the blackboard to your iPad.

There is something timeless and authentic about a piece of chalk on a blackboard. But achieving that organic, dusty, and tactile feel in digital art can be frustratingly difficult. Most "chalk" brushes look like flat grey lines, they lack the soul of the original medium.


Welcome to the Chalkboard Artist’s Toolkit. We’ve captured the raw essence of traditional chalk to give you a versatile set of tools that mimic real-world textures. Whether you are designing a rustic cafe menu, creating educational content, or sketching a moody piece of art, these brushes provide the same satisfying grit you'd find in a real classroom.


From the coarse texture of a fresh piece of chalk to the soft blur of a smudge, this kit covers every stroke you need.
