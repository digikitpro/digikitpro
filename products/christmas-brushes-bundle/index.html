Installation:

– Ensure you have Procreate 5 on your ipad

– Unzip your file (free download of Zipviewer can be installed on your iPad)

– Mac User: Airdrop .brushset to your iPad

– Windows User: Use Dropbox to transfer .brushset. In your Files app on your iPad, navigate your saved file. Tap on .brushset then click on (…) icon and Export. Open In and tap Copy to Procreate.

– The brush pack will automatically be installed.
