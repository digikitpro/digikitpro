About This Product

Stop painting with grey brushes. Start drawing with raw carbon.

Traditional charcoal is prized for its grit, its deep blacks, and its ability to smudge and blend with a visceral, tactile energy. Most digital charcoal brushes fail because they are simply "grey brushes" with a soft edge; they lack the "tooth" of the paper and the crumbly, organic behavior of real compressed charcoal. The result is art that looks sterile and lifeless.

The Professional Charcoal Studio for Procreate was engineered to eliminate this digital sterility.

This kit is a meticulous simulation of traditional carbon media. We have captured the exact behavior of charcoal on heavy-grain paper, providing you with the raw power and unpredictability of a real studio experience. Whether you are creating a dramatic chiaroscuro portrait, a moody architectural study, or a rough conceptual sketch, these brushes provide the depth, dimension, and grit required for a professional finish.

From bold, broad strokes that dominate the canvas to delicate, precise lines for fine detailing, this system allows you to blend, smudge, and layer with a natural fluidity that mimics the physical world. You get the raw energy of charcoal without the dust and the cleanup.
