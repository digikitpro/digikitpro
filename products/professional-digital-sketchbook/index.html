About This Product

The tactile feel of a traditional sketchbook. The power of a digital studio.

For many artists, the digital canvas feels too sterile. The lines are too perfect, the background is too flat, and the experience lacks the organic "soul" of a physical sketchbook. When the environment feels artificial, the creativity often follows.

The Digital Sketchbook for Procreate was engineered to solve this.

This is more than a brush set; it is a complete tactile environment. We have meticulously combined a diverse range of sketching tools with high-fidelity paper textures to replicate the experience of graphite and ink on raw paper. Whether you are drafting a professional concept, studying anatomy, or capturing a spontaneous doodle, this system provides the grip, texture, and dimension needed to make your work feel authentic.

The integration of seamless paper textures and torn-paper stamps allows you to build a composition that has depth and character. You no longer have to choose between the convenience of Procreate and the aesthetic of a traditional sketchbook. You can now have both.

What is Included in the Digital Sketchbook:

  • The Sketching Suite: A comprehensive range of brushes for everything from razor-sharp precision lines to bold, expressive strokes.
  • Torn Paper Stamps: Professional-grade stamps used to add organic edges, dimension, and a "scrapbook" feel to your compositions.
  • The Paper Archive: 13 high-resolution, seamless paper textures that provide the essential "tooth" and grain of real-world sketching paper.
  • Workflow Optimized: Fully calibrated for Apple Pencil pressure and tilt, ensuring an intuitive and natural drawing experience.
