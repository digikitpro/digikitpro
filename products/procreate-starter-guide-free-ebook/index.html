About This Product

The Procreate Portrait Starter Guide is a free PDF for artists starting portrait studies on iPad. It introduces a four-phase sequence: structure, value, surface and finish, alongside canvas setup and observation exercises.

Work through proportion landmarks, light and shadow, skin texture and edges at your own pace. The listing includes a practice plan and checklists, and says the exercises work with Procreate’s built-in brushes.

The guide stands on its own and does not require a paid kit. If you later want a longer paid coursebook, explore the Procreate Portrait Masterclass.
