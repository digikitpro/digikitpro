About This Product

The Procreate Portrait Starter Guide is our free eBook for artists starting portraits in Procreate: a clear visual sequence that takes you from a blank canvas to a structured, well-lit head study, in plain language, with exercises that fit in twenty minutes a day.

Most beginners do not lack talent, they lack a sequence. This guide gives you one: the four-phase portrait map (structure, value, surface, finish), clean canvas and layer setup, observation training, proportion landmarks, value groups, skin and texture fundamentals, edges, the finish, and the common beginner mistakes with the fix for each one.

No fluff and no pressure: every technique works with Procreate's built-in brushes. And when you are ready for the full system, The Complete Procreate Portrait Masterclass picks up exactly where this guide ends.
