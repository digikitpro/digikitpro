About This Product

The Complete Procreate Portrait Masterclass is a listed 107-page PDF coursebook in 15 chapters. It covers reference, construction, values, skin, features, hair and edges, then walks through a complete portrait project.

FAQ

Do I need DigiKitPro brushes to follow it?

No. The coursebook is brush-agnostic: every technique works with Procreate's built-in brushes, and DigiKitPro kits are optional accelerators matched to specific problems.

How is it different from the free Starter Guide?

The Starter Guide gives you the sequence and your first structured head study. The Masterclass takes the same sequence to a finished, polished portrait: audits, a complete project, troubleshooting, checklists and a four-week plan.
