About This Product

The Complete Procreate Portrait Masterclass is our 107-page visual coursebook: 15 chapters that take you from a blank canvas to a finished, believable portrait, teaching a repeatable workflow you can audit and reuse on every portrait after.

It is built on one idea: a portrait fails or succeeds at specific, fixable stages. Inside: the six-stage execution workflow, the Five-Thing Read, construction and proportion that survive difficult angles, the five value families, skin in depth (temperature, sampling audits, colour breaks, blending restraint), lines and edges, features chapter by chapter, hair and surfaces, one complete portrait project painted step by step, troubleshooting for the six classic failures, a final checklist, a four-week practice plan, and a resource map with clickable links to every tool mentioned.

Brush-agnostic: every technique works with Procreate's built-in brushes, and DigiKitPro kits are optional accelerators, never required. New here? Start with the free Portrait Starter Guide; this coursebook picks up exactly where it ends.
