About This Product

Start with a sketch and keep the tools for the rest of the portrait close at hand. Portrait Mastery Kit groups 46 brushes into seven stages: sketch, ink, blend, hair, detail, texture and finish. Four paper textures are included too.

New to portraits? Begin with a sketch brush and a blender, then introduce detail and texture as you practise. If you already have a workflow, reach for the hair, texture and finishing tools where your painting needs them. The brushes support your decisions; they do not replace practice or Procreate's built-in tools.

Choose this kit for a portrait-focused workflow. If you mainly want pores, freckles and textured blending, explore the separate Portrait Skin Brushes.

FAQ

Is this a good starting point for portrait beginners?

Yes. Start with the sketch and blend brushes and add texture and detail as you practise. The kit supplies tools, not a step-by-step course; the free Portrait Starter Guide teaches a useful starting workflow.

How is this different from the Portrait Skin Brushes?

Portrait Mastery is a 46-brush sketch-to-finish portrait kit with four paper textures. Portrait Skin Brushes is a separate 19-brush set focused on skin texture, freckles, pores and blending.

Is Portrait Mastery included in the Ultimate Portrait Mastery Bundle?

The Ultimate Portrait Mastery Bundle lists the line-art, skin, hair-stamp and glitter kits. The 46-brush Portrait Mastery Kit is not listed among those four products; check the bundle contents before buying.

What do I need to use the files?

An iPad with Procreate 5 or later. An Apple Pencil is recommended for pressure and tilt; the files are not for Photoshop or other drawing apps.

How do I install the brushes?

Download the ZIP from Payhip, tap it in the iPad Files app to extract it, and tap the .brushset file to import it into Procreate. Paper textures are separate files.

How are delivery and refunds handled?

Payhip handles checkout and digital delivery. Sales are final once files have been downloaded; contact DigiKitPro if a file has a technical problem.
