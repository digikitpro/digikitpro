About This Product

When a portrait looks a little too smooth, small changes in texture can make the surface feel more considered. This 19-brush Procreate set brings together tools for pores, freckles, wrinkles and textured blending, along with sketch, base, highlight and detail brushes.

Build the large shapes and values first, then use texture sparingly on a separate layer. Beginners can start with a blender and one texture brush; intermediate artists can combine the detail brushes with their existing painting workflow. The amount of detail and the final result are up to you.

This is a focused skin set, not the same product as the 46-brush Portrait Mastery Kit. It is also listed as one of the four kits in the Ultimate Portrait Mastery Bundle, so compare the contents before buying both.
