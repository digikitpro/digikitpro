Quick guide

How to set up your card

  1. Pick a card or print preset: Choose 5×7 Holiday Card (A7), A2 Folded Card, 4×6 Postcard, Gift Tag, or enter your own trim width and height in inches or millimeters.
  2. Choose your print bleed and safe margin: Select the bleed required by your printer (0.125 in or 3 mm per edge is a common starting point) and a safe inset margin for lettering.
  3. Create your Procreate canvas using the Bleed Canvas pixels: Open Procreate, tap the + icon to create a custom canvas, and enter the Bleed Canvas width and height in pixels at your chosen DPI.
  4. Keep greetings and borders inside the safe zone: Extend background patterns (like knit textures or paper) all the way to the bleed edge, and keep lettering and key stamps inside the safe margin.

Standard Greeting Card & Print Sizes at 300 DPI

When designing greeting cards, postcards or KDP interiors in Procreate, setting your pixel dimensions at 300 DPI with bleed before your first brush stroke prevents blurry upscaling later. Below are common starting dimensions—always verify the exact template from your print shop.

  • 5×7 in Greeting Card (A7): 1500×2100 px trim · 1575×2175 px with 0.125 in bleed per side.
  • 4.25×5.5 in Folded Note Card (A2): 1275×1650 px trim · 1350×1725 px with 0.125 in bleed per side.
  • 4×6 in Postcard / Photo Card: 1200×1800 px trim · 1275×1875 px with 0.125 in bleed per side.
  • 5.25×5.25 in Square Holiday Card: 1575×1575 px trim · 1650×1650 px with 0.125 in bleed per side.
  • A6 International Card (105×148 mm): 1240×1748 px trim · 1311×1819 px with 3 mm bleed per side.
  • A5 Greeting Card / Print (148×210 mm): 1748×2480 px trim · 1819×2551 px with 3 mm bleed per side.
  • 2×3.5 in Holiday Gift Tag: 600×1050 px trim · 675×1125 px with 0.125 in bleed per side.
  • 6×9 in KDP Interior Page: 1800×2700 px trim · 1875×2775 px with 0.125 in bleed on outer edges.

Straight answers

Common questions

What pixel size is a 5×7 greeting card in Procreate at 300 DPI?

At 300 DPI, a 5×7 inch card is 1500×2100 pixels at trim size. With a standard 0.125 inch bleed on all four edges (5.25×7.25 inches total), set your Procreate canvas to 1575×2175 pixels at 300 DPI. Confirm your print shop's exact bleed specification before printing.

How much bleed should I add to a Christmas card in Procreate?

Many US printers ask for 0.125 inches (1/8 inch) of bleed on each side, which adds 0.25 inches to both width and height (75 pixels total per dimension at 300 DPI). Many European and ISO printers ask for 3 mm of bleed on each side (6 mm total). Always check your printer's template.

What is the difference between trim size, bleed area and safe zone?

Trim size is the final cut size of the card. The bleed area extends past the trim line so background art or seamless knit patterns reach the edge without white slivers after cutting. The safe zone sits inside the trim line so lettering, names and border frames are not accidentally clipped.

What size is an A2 or A6 greeting card in pixels at 300 DPI?

A US A2 card (4.25×5.5 inches) is 1275×1650 pixels at trim, or 1350×1725 pixels with 0.125 inch bleed per side. An international A6 card (105×148 mm) is 1240×1748 pixels at trim, or 1311×1819 pixels with 3 mm bleed per side at 300 DPI.

Which Procreate brushes work well for printable holiday cards and gift tags?

Stamp brushes, seamless knit pattern brushes and washi tape brushes let you test card layouts on separate layers and recolour motifs cleanly.
