Privacy Policy

Overview

DigiKitPro ("we", "our") operates this content website and sells digital products through Payhip. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Email and delivery details: This site currently has no newsletter signup provider. Payhip may ask for an email address when delivering a free or paid product.
  • Purchase data: all payments and order data are processed by Payhip on their secure checkout. We never see or store your payment card details. See Payhip's own privacy policy for how they process order data.

Analytics & cookies

This is a static website with no server of our own, but it is not analytics-free, and we would rather tell you plainly than imply otherwise.

  • Google Analytics 4: the Google tag measures page and product activity and may set cookies and process your IP address. We do not load it if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, or if you opt out below. You can also use Google's opt-out add-on.
  • When site analytics is enabled, what we measure: page and product views, clicks on buy, free-download and optional recommendation links, and search-query length. These clicks are not proof that a purchase or download completed. Search text and URL query strings are excluded from the site's custom events. If a link has campaign tags, basic source, medium, campaign and optional content or ID labels may be sent to Google for attribution. The optional feedback collector is not configured on this site.
  • Advertising: the optional Pinterest advertising tag is not loaded unless configured by the site owner.
  • When measurement is enabled, a first-party summary of event counts is kept in your browser's localStorage; the summary itself is not uploaded. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Google Translate (the globe button in the header) is loaded from Google only when you choose a language. Google sets its own googtrans cookie to remember that choice. Do not use the button if you prefer not to contact Google.
  • Payhip handles all payments. Checkout happens on payhip.com under Payhip's own privacy policy; we never see or store your card details.

Turn site analytics off · Turn site analytics back on. Your choice is stored in this browser. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control still take precedence.

Your rights

For questions or requests about data, use the contact page. For information held by Payhip, see Payhip's privacy policy.
