# DigiKitPro SEO Content Roadmap — September 2026

## Research Summary

### Current Procreate Trends (2026)
- **Authenticity economy**: audiences value visible human authorship over AI-generated imagery
- **Hybrid aesthetics**: blending traditional techniques (watercolor, gouache, charcoal) with digital precision
- **Micro-animations**: Procreate Dreams gaining traction for short-form content
- **Student/beginner demand**: massive ongoing demand for structured Procreate learning paths
- **Portrait painting**: consistently the #1 search category — skin, hair, eyes, shading, lips
- **Layer management**: Alpha Lock, Clipping Mask, Layer Mask — high search volume, high confusion
- **Shortcuts & gestures**: evergreen demand from beginners and intermediate artists
- **Brush organization**: growing problem as brush collections grow
- **Social sharing**: time-lapse, Instagram, Pinterest content creation

### Existing Blog Coverage (18 articles)
- Brush buying guides: anime, beginners, line art, portraits
- Tutorials: realistic skin, skin textures, watercolor, hair, traditional look, blending, portrait workflow, tattoo, Christmas, Halloween
- Technical: install brushes, canvas size/DPI, stamp vs painting
