# Guide and search-copy refresh — 29 October 2026

## Scope and evidence

The supplied Search Console Web export for **29 September–26 October 2026** reported 172 total impressions and no clicks. The page rows were HTTP URLs: the brush-choice guide had 70 impressions, the anime guide 43, the traditional-art guide 22 and the skin guide 14. The row totals and site total are aggregated differently. These are small historical counts, not proof of a particular ranking, cause of low clicks, or current HTTPS indexing. The beginner guide was included for its relevance to the free-resource journey, not because its impressions were established.

## Changes

- Five guides now give a short answer before the cover, offer practical exercises, and distinguish built-in, free and optional paid tools. The covers remain complete and are compact beside the article on wider screens. Search titles and descriptions reflect the actual page content; only these substantively edited articles received a new `modified` date.
- Nineteen relevant product titles and descriptions describe catalogued contents without embedded prices, unverified delivery promises or savings claims. Only `seoTitle` and `seoDesc` changed in the 51 product records. Existing Payhip links, prices, availability, product identities and all other catalog fields were preserved.
- The product description field also supplies Pinterest catalog descriptions: the two generated Pinterest feeds therefore have updated text on 15 paid rows, with their IDs, prices, availability, links and other fields unchanged. No campaign was launched. The Google Merchant feed uses the existing product `short` text and is unchanged.
- Normal SEO enrichment was tested on copies of all 19 edited manual records: their custom titles and descriptions were preserved. The SEO check now compares generated product/article metadata with their sources and verifies the opt-in article opening order.

## Verification and limits

Local checks on the generated site: **3,091 SEO checks**, **115 static checks**, **196 Chromium checks**, **138 gallery checks**, and **2,805 local asset references with none missing**. An analytics-disabled build passed SEO and static checks; 106 HTML files contained no Google tag bootstrap. An offline form-state test passed; it did not contact a provider. Automated axe checks found zero violations on 12 sampled JavaScript-enabled pages at 390px, not a full accessibility audit. Browser checks also covered the five guides with JavaScript on and off, 320–1440px layouts, and local free/paid resource links; Payhip and analytics were stubbed.

An initial concurrent browser run timed out waiting for the existing JavaScript-off 404 menu to become stable; two subsequent runs passed. The cause of that timeout was not established.

No real purchase, delivery, iPad import, Google indexing, rank, click-through or sales improvement was verified. Google may choose different [title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) and [snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet). Product facts are drawn from the existing catalog, not a fresh inspection of purchased files.

## Owner follow-up

1. **Check the files:** the Essential Line Art product says 43 brushes plus a bonus, but its listed groups add up to 42 plus one bonus. Confirm the actual contents before repeating a total. The search copy avoids that disputed count; existing product-page text still needs owner review.
2. **Check overlap:** the two 100-plus anime sets have similar descriptions, but the catalog does not establish whether they share brushes. Verify the files and clarify the product pages before encouraging customers to buy both.
3. **Review existing claims and evidence:** some untouched product text and cover artwork still say “instant download” or “lifetime access.” Confirm delivery/access terms and add genuine, labelled stroke demonstrations when available; this refresh does not validate those claims.
4. **After deployment and recrawl**, use the correctly verified Search Console property and consistent page, date and search-type filters to compare impressions, clicks, CTR and average position over comparable periods. Check Google-selected canonicals and HTTP/HTTPS reporting before aggregating. Low counts and unrelated changes can make short-term comparisons inconclusive; sitemap submission and requests to index do not guarantee indexing.
