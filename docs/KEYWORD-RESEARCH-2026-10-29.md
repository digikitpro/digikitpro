# Product keyword research — October 2026

Initial research and the product keyword map were saved on 29 October 2026 before product-copy work. This report was subsequently expanded with additional long-tail and competitor notes and redacted to keep private analytics outside the public repository. Scope: English-language Google discovery, considering the US and international audiences. This is a research snapshot, not proof of ranking, sales or the cause of low conversion.

## What the evidence can and cannot tell us

- **No verified October-only global or US keyword ranking is available here.** October was still in progress. The Google Trends Explore request returned HTTP 429; no Trends readings were recovered. There was no Google Ads Keyword Planner account/export available. Search Console account access was not available; the owner's earlier, privately supplied exports were reviewed locally.
- The owner's Search Console export covers the site's appearances, not all Google searches. The small query sample cannot rank market demand or establish converting keywords. It predates these changes, and Google's selected canonicals are not verified. Private figures and rows are omitted from this public repository; the owner has a separate local baseline summary.
- The third-party [RankHero keyword pages](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/procreate-brushes) supply global average estimates and historical monthly rows. RankHero's own [browser extension description](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/rankhero-tools-for-etsy-s/lfhkahikfmfbcmcjidldclckjpggbldk) describes Google monthly search volume alongside Etsy listing competition, but the public keyword pages do not explain their full volume methodology or refresh dates. Some pages say “Not enough data yet”, and their headline listing counts can differ from their FAQ counts. The figures below are **vendor estimates, not first-party October counts**; latest available months differ. Treat as directional, not precise or comprehensive. Etsy counts and Etsy difficulty are **not Google organic ranking difficulty**.
- [Keywords Everywhere's graphic-design list](https://keywordseverywhere.com/ctl/top/graphic-design-keywords) reports global monthly averages refreshed October 2026, not October-only totals. It gives “digital painting” 40,500 and “digital illustration” 22,200. These broad, largely informational terms do not measure demand for a specific Procreate product. The advertised competition on that page is Google Ads competition, not organic SEO difficulty.
- An [October brush roundup](https://graphicdesignjunction.com/2026/10/best-procreate-brushes-of-2026/) and active [Etsy product results](https://www.etsy.com/market/procreate_brushes) provide examples of wording and competing offers, **not search volumes or a ranking census**. The [Procreate handbook](https://help.procreate.com/procreate/handbook/brushes/brush-library) confirms that the app has built-in brush sets and supports importing custom brushes from Files. Built-in or free brushes can be useful; paid does not automatically mean better.
- Google describes [Trends](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365533?hl=en) as sampled, normalized relative interest, not search counts, and [Keyword Planner](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en) averages and rounds historical estimates. Google controls its displayed [title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) and [snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet).

## Strategy and differentiation

- Use a precise product phrase plus the real kit name rather than repeating “best”, “free” or every adjacent brush category. The priority is fit and purchase intent, not the largest generic volume.
- Portrait Mastery is the seven-stage portrait workflow; Portrait Skin is the focused texture set; Artista is the broad illustration kit. The portrait bundle lists Skin as a component: ask customers to compare before buying both. A brush supports practice; it does not replace it.
- Distinguish sketch/liner, marker, charcoal and watercolor products by their listed media. Distinguish the large libraries by their catalog names and stated counts rather than promising non-overlap. The two anime listings describe very similar sets; file overlap is **unknown**, so do not assert they are different, complementary, or safe to purchase together.
- Free Fine Liner, Chalkboard, Color Vault and Portrait Starter Guide remain independent, useful downloads. Relevant premium products are optional next steps, not required upgrades or proof that the free item is inferior.
- Non-Procreate products retain their own intent: GoodNotes planners, Canva KDP templates, Japanese illustrations, Halloween PNGs and the Morocco guide should not receive irrelevant Procreate terms.
- Retain the 2026 dates in genuinely dated planners; do not imply 2027 compatibility. No price, discount, rating, ownership, commercial-use right, compatibility or performance claim follows from keyword popularity.

## What to obtain next

For a defensible October ranking, export **Google Keyword Planner historical monthly columns** or a named provider's monthly data for the US and relevant English-speaking markets, with the source, date, region and network visible. Review both alongside real paid orders and product-page traffic before prioritizing further edits or paying for ads.
