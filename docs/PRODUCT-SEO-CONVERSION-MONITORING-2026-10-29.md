# Conversion recommendations and 30-day monitoring plan

Prepared 29 October 2026. These are hypotheses and operating instructions, **not evidence of an uplift or a diagnosed conversion problem**. The proposed copy is on a review branch and has not been deployed. Day 0 below is a future owner-authorized deployment, not the date of this report. Do not launch a promotion, alter checkout, collect marketing consent or publish the branch without separate approval.

## Definitions for the dashboard

Keep product IDs, date ranges, timezone, channel definitions and currency consistent. Prefer session-scoped rates only when numerator and denominator can be joined to the **same** session and consent permits measurement.

| KPI | Definition and source | Caveats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GSC clicks, impressions, CTR and position | Web Search Performance report, correct domain/property, by canonical product page and separately by query/country/device. CTR = clicks ÷ impressions. | Use comparable full days with reporting lag. Privacy/aggregation can hide queries. Position is the average position of appearances, not a stable rank. Verify HTTP/HTTPS and Google-selected canonical in URL Inspection. |
| Organic product sessions | GA4 sessions attributed to Organic Search landing on a product page, split paid/free and product slug. | GA4 and GSC use different counting and attribution; do not expect matching totals. Record consent/opt-outs and cross-domain configuration. |
| Paid-product view → outbound purchase-intent rate | Sessions with `product_buy_click` for a paid product ÷ sessions with a view of that same product, if session-level reporting can match both consistently. Also show raw views/clicks. | This is a click-through proxy, not checkout completion or conversion. Separate free-download clicks and optional upgrade clicks. |
| Add-to-cart rate | Sessions with a **validated actual Payhip add-to-cart** ÷ eligible product-view sessions, where the checkout supports and exposes that event. | Current storefront outbound clicks are not add-to-cart. Until instrumentation is validated, report **unavailable**, not zero. |
| Checkout start and completion | Real Payhip checkout starts and completed paid orders; use Payhip reports as the primary operational record. | Confirm product-level definitions, cancellations, refunds and cross-domain continuity. A checkout click or thank-you URL alone proves neither. |
| Purchases and net revenue | Confirmed paid Payhip orders and net revenue, consistently accounting for refunds, taxes/fees and currency; show gross and refund treatment separately. | GA4 purchase/revenue is secondary until purchase event, deduplication and attribution are validated. Exclude free orders from paid-order counts. Do not infer customer-level joins without appropriate consent. |
| Qualified free → paid interest | Paid recommendation clicks following free-product exposure, then independently confirmed paid orders if attribution is available. | Free-resource access is independent; a recommendation URL can be visited without a download. Do not claim that a click proves a free download or a later purchase. |
| Page experience and support | Real-user LCP/INP/CLS by relevant device and product where available; download/import problems and refund/support contacts from owner reports. | Local browser timing is not field performance. Aggregate and anonymize support information. |

### Comparison and rollback rules

- Define the baseline as the **28 complete days immediately before the authorized release**, not the mixed-length historical snapshots above. Compare with 28 complete post-release days after reporting lag; optionally repeat excluding the first week for indexing delay, but label that analysis separately. For a Day 0 deployment, the fully settled 28-day report may fall just after Day 30.
- Segment by existing paid product, free product, device and country; prioritize consistent denominators and absolute counts. Watch seasonal products separately, especially Halloween/Christmas and dated 2026 planners. Do not compare aggregate position alone as if it were a fixed rank.
- Keep a change log and, where possible, use a matched holdout or a pre-agreed controlled test without withholding essential corrections. Pre/post differences alone do not establish causation. No traffic or sale targets are fabricated here.
- If an approved release causes a broken checkout link, missing page, wrong price, inaccessible free resource, major consent issue, or confirmed feed/policy problem, pause further changes, document the evidence and prepare a **reviewed revert** through the normal branch/PR process with owner authorization. Do not overwrite customer data or change live settings as an improvised rollback.
