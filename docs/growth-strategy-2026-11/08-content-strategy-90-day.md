## Per-article skeleton (apply to all 12)

- **Meta description** ≤155 chars, benefit-first, one keyword, no clickbait.
- **H2 outline**: the 7-step structure already used on the skin article — problem → principle → pass-by-pass → common mistakes → FAQ (HowTo schema) → CTA.
- **Internal links**: exactly 1 product, 1 related article, 1 freebie per post; anchor text = descriptive (not "click here").
- **Media**: ≥4 original process images (before/after, layer stack, stroke sample sheet) with descriptive alt text; 1 vertical 9:16 pin-crop per post.
- **CTA**: context-matched (Skin kit → article 1; Bundle → article 3/5; Freebies → articles 2/10; Drills → article 12; Course/Masterclass → System bundle).
- **Refresh loop** (weeks 1–4): re-date, re-verify counts, add schema check to articles 1/3/4/5/9.

## Publishing cadence & ops

- Week 1–2: refreshes 1/4; publish 2. Week 3–4: refresh 3/5/9; publish 1-adjacent support piece (Skin "checklist" PDF landing note). Month 2: articles 6, 7, 8 (+ pins ×3 each). Month 3: articles 10, 11, 12; results review; double down on the 2 best-CTR posts.
- Each post gets: IndexNow ping (Bing/Yandex), one Pinterest pin, one X/Facebook share, and an internal-link insertion into the most relevant category page on rebuild.
- Distribution without ads until baseline exists; revisit paid ($5/day Pinterest boosting of the best pin) only after 30 days of data.

## What success looks like (no promises)

Directional targets to *review* at day 30/60/90, not to promise: indexed pages = published count; median position of target keywords improves by ≥5 places or enters top-30; ≥1 article enters top-10 for its long-tail; MC CTR for promoted products ≥ 0.8% (current baseline **unknown** — capture first); email signups from freebies tracked in Payhip/ConvertKit stats.
