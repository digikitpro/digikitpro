## What 2026 Pinterest search rewards (validated against this niche)

- Process/before-after imagery outperforms stroke sheets; listicle-style pins ("5 fixes for plastic skin") earn saves; portrait aesthetics dominate the arts vertical; consistency (weekly) beats burst posting. 1. **YouTube Shorts + 1 long-form/month** — the DrifterStudio funnel pattern (free tutorial → brushset purchase). Lowest cost authority available; reuse the portrait before/after as 35–55s verticals; long-form = "full portrait process with Portrait Mastery". Subscribe-funnel to freebies (link in description).
2. **Reddit (value-only)** — answer skin/brush questions in r/ProCreate with genuine texture advice; link free resources only when directly relevant; never paste product links unprompted (you will be spam-flagged — correctly).

5. **Email** — freebie-gated list from Color Vault/Starter Guide; 2 emails/month (one tutorial, one offer); this is the compounding asset once Pinterest traffic starts.
