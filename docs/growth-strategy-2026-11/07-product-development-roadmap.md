# Phase 8 — Competitive Product Development Roadmap (2026-11-08)

Ranked by (evidence strength × fit × speed). Prices anchored to competitor evidence, not invented: Etsy impulse $4.80–8, Creative Market considered $14–50 [Verified-public], own-site authority $19–97 (Lisa Glanz $16–21, RetroSupply $97, True Grit $15–107).

## P1 — ship within 30 days

**1. Portrait Drills — practice worksheets PDF** — Target: portrait students, self-learners. Problem: no structured practice path (every competitor ships tools, not reps). Evidence: Reddit portrait threads ask for *how to improve*, not more brushes ([r/ProCreate tips thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/ProCreate/comments/1ox2zby/tips_for_more_realistic_rendering_on_procreate/)). Alternatives: none direct; closest = Lisa Glanz embedded tutorial (16-page) inside a $19 set. Differentiation: standalone drills + answer key + license. Contents: 25 worksheets (value ladders, edge control, eye/nose/lip construction, skin-texture passes) + 4 practice canvases; PDF + print. Price: **$6** (single) / **$12** with Portrait Mastery Kit. Keywords: procreate portrait practice, procreate practice sheets, how to draw portraits procreate. Cross-sell: Skin kit, Mastery Kit, Masterclass eBook. Confidence: **High**.

**2. Facial Features Kit — eyes, brows, lips, noses** — Target: portrait artists stuck on features (most-asked category in portrait threads). Problem: mega-bundle stamps don't fit; quality varies; no guidance. Evidence: 2026 listicle focus on eye stamps ([brusher.art](https://www.brusher.art/procreate/best-brushes-2026/)); Kimmyw bundles monetise features; Mega Stamps listing weak reviews (4.5★/3). Alternatives: ArtworkbyGabrielle (lashes/makeup $4.80), Kimmyw brow stamps ($12, inside $50 bundle). Differentiation: 3 task groups (shape/render/polish) + mini-guide PDF + commercial-use clarity. Contents: 24 stamps + 12 brushes + 6-page placement guide. Price: **$9**. Keywords: procreate eye brushes, procreate lip brushes, portrait stamps procreate. Cross-sell: Ultimate bundle, Skin kit, Face-forward bundle of the three. Confidence: **Medium-High**.

**3. "Complete Portrait System" bundle (Masterclass + Mastery Kit + Skin Kit)** — Target: buyers who would otherwise buy piecemeal. Problem: decision fatigue; no guided path. Evidence: bundle psychology validated by RetroSupply ($97, 395 reviews) and Lisa Glanz (tutorial-included sets earn reviews). Alternatives: same as #1/2 components à la carte. Differentiation: one download, one install, one curriculum; honest math on the bundles page; anchors value of the $5–8 à la carte items. Contents: everything in the 3 components + 30-min "your first textured portrait" walkthrough PDF. Price: **$22** (vs $32 à la carte — stated honestly). Keywords: procreate portrait kit, procreate portrait bundle. Cross-sell: everything portrait. Confidence: **High**.

## P2 — 30–60 days

**4. Portrait Tone Deck — 60 palettes + 4 blenders** — Target: artists painting varied skin tones; gift buyers. Problem: flat, narrow default palettes; no licensing-clear commercial-skin-tone deck. Evidence: palette demand visible across listicles; free Color Vault is DigiKitPro's top lead magnet — paid upgrade is the natural ladder rung. Alternatives: vendor-unknown palette packs on CM ($10-15). Differentiation: undertone-first taxonomy (cool/warm/olive/deep) + blender pairing notes; matches line-art + skin assets already owned. Price: **$7**; bundle with Skin kit **$10**. Keywords: procreate skin tone palette, procreate portrait color palette. Confidence: **Medium**.

**5. Curls & Braids Vol. 1 — hair stamp expansion** — Target: curly/coily hair subjects, character artists. Problem: straight-hair stamps dominate; curls hard to draw repeatedly. Evidence: Kimmyw Curls & Braids are core to a verified 1,106-sale bundle [Verified-public]; 2026 listicles call braid stamps "a lifesaver" ([bebrushes.com](https://bebrushes.com/35-best-procreate-brushes/)). Alternatives: Kimmyw (CM, $14/vol). Differentiation: DigiKitPro integration with existing 160-stamp kit; PDF "hairline continuity at the neck" (addresses the exact complaint seen in the wild). Price: **$8** (add-on; bundle pricing with Ultimate). Confidence: **Medium**.

**6. Ink Drift — analog-fusion ink set (5 brushes)** — Target: Inktober-style illustrators, analog enthusiasts. Problem: 2026 "perfect gradient" fatigue; desire for bleed/spatter/rough-ink character. Evidence: [artcoastdesign 2026 trend note](https://artcoastdesign.com/blog/procreate-ink-brushes-trends-2026) (vendor blog, directional); Reddit texture demand. Alternatives: Sinix/Jingsketch freebies (no commercial polish). Differentiation: 5 brushes + 1-page "ink bleed recipe" card; low price = low risk trial. Price: **$5**. Keywords: procreate ink brushes, procreate ink bleed. Confidence: **Low-Medium** (validate via Pinterest save-rate before scaling).
