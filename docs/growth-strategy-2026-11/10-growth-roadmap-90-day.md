**Weekly 15-minute Monday review** (paste into a doc; keep 13 rows of history):

1. MC: impressions, clicks, CTR; list any disapproval/warning; top/bottom 3 products.
2. GSC: last 28 days vs previous 28; top movers (query/page); any "Crawled – not indexed" spikes; new queries entering.
3. Pinterest: pins published, outbound clicks, saves; best pin of the week.
4. Store: orders, refunds, top product, freebie signups.
5. Actions: 3 items max for the coming week; each mapped to a task ID above.

**Segmentation rules:** always separate (a) Google organic web, (b) Google Shopping/free listings, (c) images/video, (d) direct/email, (e) Pinterest. Never blend into one "traffic" number. Paid is zero until explicitly launched — keep it that way until baseline exists.

**Anti-goals:** do not add thin articles to hit a quota; do not chase head terms; do not buy backlinks; do not inflate claims ("lifetime access" is banned in this repo); do not mark a task done without its QA/evidence attached.

**Stop/continue gates at day 30/60/90:** if MC CTR < 0.5% on promoted items → revisit images/titles before more copy; if a target keyword hasn't moved into top-50 → swap target page or de-prioritise the article; if refund rate > 5% on any product → pause promotion, fix expectation gap first.
