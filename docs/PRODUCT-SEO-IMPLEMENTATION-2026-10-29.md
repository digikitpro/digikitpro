### Feed-specific changes need owner review

The existing Pinterest feed assigned **every product**, including planners and a travel PDF, the Google taxonomy category “Graphic Design & Illustration Software.” The review build omits this optional category rather than asserting an unsupported classification; the 47 paid Pinterest rows and their product-type labels remain. Pinterest documents this field as optional. Removal may change feed diagnostics or distribution; review the connected account before publication. [Pinterest catalog fields](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/before-you-get-started-with-catalogs)

The existing shared Google Merchant feed contained three $0 non-book downloads and the paid Morocco PDF travel guide. Google's [product data specification](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112) generally disallows zero prices except specific cases, while [Shopping policy](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150006) excludes digital books from Shopping ads but distinguishes free listings. The review build conservatively holds out all four free products and the Morocco guide from this common feed, and still excludes the two Procreate eBooks. **45 paid, non-book candidates remain; their eligibility is not certified.** Free organic pages and Payhip links are unchanged. Before deployment, decide with the owner whether a separate destination-specific feed is appropriate; a connected feed could remove previously submitted items. No Merchant Center or Pinterest account was accessed, no ad was created, and no price was changed.
