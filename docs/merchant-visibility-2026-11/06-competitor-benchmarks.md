## Title & category conventions to copy (not names)

- **Task-first constructions**: "Procreate [Task] Brushes | [USP]" — matches how Google reads Shopping queries (and how our 2026-10-29 rewrite was built). Keep DigiKitPro's `product-type-first` formula: it already matches the Etsy-verified pattern.
- No GTIN/MPN = both marketplaces and MC ignore identifier rules positively (none assigned).
- All 10 use **visible review counters** on marketplaces; MC free listings can surface review-rich shopping results — our in-site review gap (Phase 4) directly suppresses CTR vs these.

## Categories & pricing frame

MC categories for our catalog (500046 / 8022, reviewed nodes) / Google "overrides" caveat: competitors on Etsy/CM never face this — one more reason their counters display; nothing to copy except *clarity*: accurate node, complete highlights, review parity, honest images. Price: DigiKitPro undercuts #4/#5/#6 everywhere on like-for-like portrait kits, except Gabrielle at $4.80 impulse; our Skin Kit at $7 is defensible with the before/after proof.

## Net positioning statement

"The technically honest portrait studio for Procreate — demonstrations over counters, guides over listings, bundles over singles." Every competitor is either **trust-rich/cheap** (Gabrielle/Celine) or **authority-rich/expensive** (RetroSupply). DigiKitPro wins by being **evidence-rich/affordable**: real before/after art, explicit counts, guides with workflow, $5–25 ladder ending at the $25 Complete Portrait System (PR #82).
