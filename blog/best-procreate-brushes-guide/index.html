Best Procreate Brushes: Free Sets, Styles & How to Choose

By the DigiKitPro studio · Published 2026-11-10

The best Procreate brushes are the ones that help you make the mark your artwork needs—not the pack with the biggest number. Start with Procreate’s built-in brushes or a free set, then choose a specialist collection only when you can name the gap in your workflow.

If you want to try a download first, DigiKitPro’s Free Brushes page has a fine-liner brush set and a chalk brush set. It also has a free Color Vault, but that is a collection of color palettes—not brushes.

This guide uses DigiKitPro products as examples of different workflows, not as an independent ranking of every brush on the market. Contents and requirements can change, so check the linked product page before downloading or buying.

What makes a Procreate brush useful?

A Procreate brush is a digital drawing tool inside the Procreate app. Its shape, grain, stroke behavior, texture, and pressure response affect the marks it makes. The right choice depends on your canvas, stylus, subject, and style. A simple liner you can control may help more than a large library of brushes you rarely open.

Before adding a new set, draw the same curve, short hatch, and shaded patch with brushes you already own. Compare the marks at the size your finished work will be seen. If you can identify a specific need—cleaner taper, more visible grain, or a softer wash—you have a practical reason to look for another brush.

How to download and import Procreate brushes on iPad

Most DigiKitPro brush products are described as Procreate files for iPad. If the download arrives as a ZIP, extract it in the Files app first. Then locate the .brushset or .brush file and open it in Procreate, or use Brush Library → + → Import from Files. Check the Brush Library for the imported brush or set, then test a mark on a blank layer.

Do not try to import a ZIP, PDF, or .swatches palette as a brush. A .brushset is a Procreate format; do not assume it will work in Photoshop or another drawing app. Procreate’s official import instructions explain the current import options.

For screenshots, ZIP extraction steps, and common fixes, follow our complete guide to installing Procreate brushes on iPad.

Free brushes or a paid brush set?

Start free if you are learning, testing a file, or practicing a mark. Consider a paid set when a repeated task is slowing down work you already do—for example, if you need a more organized pencil-and-ink workflow, a dedicated watercolor collection, or a specific texture style.

Before choosing, check:

  • The marks: look for stroke examples that match the way you draw.
  • The contents: confirm what is included and whether the set is organized by task.
  • The file and app: check the format, iPad requirements, download size, and compatibility on the product page.
  • The license: DigiKitPro’s Terms of Service cover use of finished artwork and prohibit redistributing the source files. Review the terms and product page for your use case.

You do not need hundreds of brushes to make good art. Keep the tools you use, and add another set only when it solves a clear problem. For a beginner-friendly practice plan, see Best Procreate Brushes for Beginners.

Want to make or customize your own Procreate brushes?

Procreate’s Brush Studio lets you change settings such as shape, grain, taper, rendering, and Apple Pencil response. If you want to customize instead of download, start with our Procreate brush settings guide. Finding a ready-made brush and building your own are different tasks; you do not need to customize a brush to use Procreate well.

FAQ

Where can I get free Procreate brushes?

DigiKitPro’s Free Brushes page lists a free Fine Liner brush set and a free Chalkboard brush set. It also includes a color-palette collection and a portrait PDF guide; those are free resources, but they are not brush sets.

How do I add brushes to Procreate?

On iPad, extract a ZIP if needed, then open the .brushset or .brush file in Procreate. You can also use Brush Library → + → Import from Files. See the full installation guide if the file does not appear.

What are the best free Procreate brushes?

It depends on what you want to practice. The Free Fine Liner Set is for linework and textured marks; the Chalkboard Artist’s Toolkit is for chalk-style marks. Neither is required to learn drawing, and Procreate’s built-in brushes are also a good place to start.

Do Procreate brushes work in Photoshop?

A .brushset is made for Procreate, not Photoshop. Check the product’s listed formats and software requirements before downloading; do not assume a brush file transfers between apps.

How many Procreate brushes do I need?

There is no required number. A sketch brush, a liner, a painting brush, and one texture tool can be enough to explore a workflow. Add more only when you know what you want them to do.

Can I use Procreate brushes for commercial artwork?

Check the brush creator’s license. DigiKitPro’s Terms of Service allow personal and commercial use of finished artwork unless a product page says otherwise, but do not allow reselling or sharing the brush files themselves.
